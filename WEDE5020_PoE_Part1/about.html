Our story

About Joss Sweet Crumbs

A small bakery built around fresh food, creativity and community.

History

From a love of baking to a bakery

Joss Sweet Crumbs Bakery began with a simple idea: make comforting baked goods that bring people together. What started with home-style recipes grew into a bakery offering everyday treats, celebration cakes and catering options.

Our approach remains personal. We value fresh preparation, thoughtful presentation and listening to what customers need for their occasions.

What guides us

Mission, vision and values

Mission

To provide delicious, fresh and thoughtfully made bakery products while giving customers friendly and dependable service.

Vision

To grow into a trusted local bakery known for quality, creativity and memorable customer experiences.

Values

Quality, creativity, cleanliness, reliability, customer care and a genuine love of baking.
